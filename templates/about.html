About Us- AI Fake Job Detector

Empowering job seekers with advanced AI technology to fight recruitment fraud.

🎯Our Mission

Smart AI Detection for a Fraud-Free Job Market.


In the digital age, fake job postings and internship scams are rising rapidly, targeting vulnerable students and job seekers.

AI Fake Job Detector is an advanced system designed to identify and filter out fraudulent job offers before they can harm anyone. Using machine learning algorithms, our system analyzes descriptions, requirements, and structural anomalies to provide a real-time risk score.

🛡️ Why Use Our System?

Identify suspicious job postings before applying.

🤖

AI-Powered Detection

Uses a trained Random Forest Machine Learning model to classify job postings as genuine or potentially fraudulent.

🔍

Suspicious Indicators

Detects warning signs such as registration fees, payment requests, guaranteed jobs, OTP requests and other suspicious patterns.

🛡️

Safety Recommendations

Provides recommendations to help users avoid financial loss and protect sensitive information.

📊

Risk Score

Generates a risk score and categorizes the job as Low, Medium or High Risk.

💾

Analysis History

Stores previous job analysis results using SQLite database for future reference.

🎓

Job & Internship

Designed to analyze both job postings and internship opportunities.

⚙️ How It Works

Simple four-step job verification process.

1

Enter Details

Enter job title, company, description, requirements and salary.

2

TF-IDF

Job information is converted into numerical features using TF-IDF.

3

AI Prediction

Random Forest analyzes the job information and predicts potential fraud.

4

Get Result

View fake probability, risk score, warnings and safety recommendations.
